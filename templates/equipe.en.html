Who Are We?

We are DD Corp, a robotics team from Gustave Eiffel high school in Armentières, passionate about innovation and technology. We take part in the FIRST LEGO LEAGUE.

What Is the First Lego League?

The First Lego League (FLL) is a competition that encourages young people to explore science, technology, engineering and mathematics through challenges based on:

  • robotics missions, where LEGO pieces are used to build a robot and its attachments.
  • and also an innovation project on a theme that changes every year, which pushes us to think about solutions to real-world problems.

Our Challenges:

  • Design and program a LEGO robot that will complete missions on the game table.
  • Develop an innovation project related to this year's theme.
  • Work as a team and respect the values of the FLL: discovery, innovation, inclusion and (most importantly!) FUN!!!!
